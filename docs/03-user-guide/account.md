# Account

> Managing your Koodook account.

---

## Profile

- **Name** --- Display name shown on the platform
- **Avatar** --- Profile picture
- **Bio** --- Short description about yourself

## Settings

- **Email** --- Change your email address
- **Password** --- Update your password
- **Language** --- Preferred interface language
- **Notifications** --- Email and in-app notification preferences

## Purchase History

View your past course purchases including:
- Course name
- Purchase date
- Payment method
- Amount paid
- Receipt
