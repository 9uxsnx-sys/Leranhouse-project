# Getting Started

> Your first steps on Koodook.

---

## Creating an Account

1. Go to the Koodook login page
2. Click **Sign Up**
3. Enter your email and create a password
4. Verify your email address
5. Complete your profile (name, avatar)

## Browsing Courses

- The **Home** page shows featured and recommended courses
- The **Courses** page lets you browse all available courses
- Use the search bar to find specific courses
- Filter by category or difficulty

## Purchasing a Course

1. Click on a course to view its details
2. Review the description, modules, and requirements
3. Click **Purchase** to proceed to payment
4. Pay via EDAHABIA or CIB card (powered by Chargily)
5. Once payment is confirmed, you can access all lessons

## Taking a Course

1. Go to your **Courses** or **Trail** page
2. Click the course you want to start
3. Navigate through modules and lessons
4. Complete activities (videos, quizzes, assignments)
5. Track your progress

## Getting Help

- Check the course description for requirements
- Use community discussions to ask questions
- Contact support for account or payment issues
